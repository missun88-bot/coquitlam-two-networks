Two Networks, One City.

Faster through the city. Further into its landscape.

From its neighbourhood streets to its forested slopes, Coquitlam invites two different journeys. Roads help us cross the city faster; footpaths lead beyond the street grid — toward creeks, mountain trails, and views reached one step at a time. Two networks, two ways to discover the same place.
